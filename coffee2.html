From Bean to Butterfly

Our Story

Papillon Brew began with a simple belief: that a single cup of coffee can turn an ordinary morning into something extraordinary. Just as a caterpillar becomes a butterfly, we believe every visit here should feel like a small metamorphosis — a pause, a breath, a moment of quiet wonder.

We partner with local farmers across Punjab for our beans, roasting each batch in small quantities to keep its character intact. Our teas are grown in the foothills we call home, and our pastries are baked fresh every morning using honey, herbs, and fruit from nearby gardens.

Sustainability isn't a footnote here — it's the soil we grow from. Compostable cups, reusable jars for our regulars, and a near zero-waste kitchen are just the beginning. "Papillon" is French for butterfly, and every choice we make asks the same quiet question: what would a butterfly do?
